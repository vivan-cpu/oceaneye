## Stakeholder testing - do at least one before 5 Nov

Pick whatever you can actually reach in 4 days:
- Email/call a marine science or remote-sensing professor (try nearby universities, IIT/NIT
  civil or ocean departments, Plaksha faculty). Send 2 screenshots and ask: "what would make this useful?"
- Fishermen / boat owners (phone call is fine): "how do you find out about oil in the water today?"
- A geography or environmental science teacher at school - let them try the app and note what confused them.
- Write to INCOIS (Hyderabad) - they run India's ocean services. Even a short reply is evidence.
Record one line of feedback and one change you made because of it. Judges like seeing that loop.

## Recording tips
- Screen record with OBS (free) or Win+G on Windows. 1080p.
- Tank: film from the side with a phone, room lights off, laser dot visible on the water.
- Run the app on the real spill once **before** recording so the image is cached (no waiting on camera).
- Keep music low. Subtitles help if audio is noisy.
